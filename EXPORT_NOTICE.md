# Export notice

This tree is a sanitised export. Receipts are dated records: flags such as `release_ready: false` inside them record the state on the day the receipt was written and were not rewritten. Hashes, sizes and file names of private artefacts and private paths are redacted in some copies. Digests of the unredacted form of any file this export rewrote, and of any earlier version of a shipped file that it would have changed or refused, are withheld and read `[redacted: withheld digest]`, because such a digest would let a reader test guesses for a redacted value. Other embedded digests are historical and were not rebound to the exported bytes. The author's commit ids of 40 hex characters in the records refer to a private development repository and do not resolve publicly. Commit ids of third-party repositories are public. Timestamps that carried a local offset are restated in UTC. A date field that repeats the date in its file name reads that date alone. Dated documents name some files that are not part of this package.

A digest of 64 hex characters ships in this tree only in two cases. It is the sha256 of a file in this tree, or it is a hash that the benchmark publishes of a rendered image, model weights, a public third-party file, a binary output of a run (an array archive or a PDF), or a position, label or corner. Every other digest of that length, such as the digest of a receipt, a log, a plan, a selection file, a script or a text written for the project, reads `[redacted: withheld digest]`.

The row inventories differ from the frozen files in three kinds of field only, and each reads the marker in place of the digest. `source_sha256` -> `render_batch.mjs` is the digest of the frozen render launcher. `excluded_fen_sha256` is the digest of the list of excluded positions. A `source_sha256` entry that names a shard manifest is the digest of the frozen manifest, which this export rewrote. In `benchmark_v2/adaptation.jsonl` the three kinds read the marker in 16000, 16000 and 16000 of 16000 rows. In `benchmark_v2/benchmark.jsonl` the three kinds read the marker in 20000, 20000 and 20000 of 20000 rows. Every other field of every row is unchanged, and the rows are the same in number and order. The shipped inventories are therefore not byte-identical to the frozen ones. `PACKAGE_INTEGRITY.json` binds the shipped bytes.

Current benchmark-data asset licensing is ready under the author's decision. See docs/records/PHUONG_RELEASE_STATUS_2026-10-12.complete.json. No family remains asset-gated. That statement covers asset licensing only: it does not record that the package was reproduced or that its publication was approved. Base weights and adapters are withheld.
